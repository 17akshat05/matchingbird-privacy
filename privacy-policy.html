Matching Bird Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 2026
Privacy Policy | Terms of Service

Welcome to Matching Bird ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, use, and safeguard your information when you access or use our mobile application and related services.

Key Privacy Highlights:
  • We collect profile details, photos, and location data to facilitate matchmaking with nearby users.
  • You can update your discovery preferences or disable location permissions at any time.
  • We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly in the app.
  • Matching Bird is strictly for adults aged 18 and older.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account & Registration: User identifier (UID), email address, and authentication tokens via Google, Email, or Guest authentication.
  • Profile Information: Name, gender, age / birth date, connection preferences, bio, interests, and profile prompt answers.
  • Photos & Media: Profile gallery photos and avatar frame selections.
  • Messages & Communications: Direct messages, chat exchanges, likes, and passes between matched users.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Geographical Location: With your permission, we collect your GPS location to calculate distances and recommend nearby matches within your chosen radius. You can also manually choose your discovery city.
  • Device & Log Information: Device model, operating system version, crash diagnostics, and activity timestamps to maintain app performance and prevent fraud.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Matchmaking & Connections: Connecting you with relevant profiles based on location, age, and interest filters.
  • Account Management: Authenticating logins, maintaining your profile, and restoring your preferences.
  • Safety & Moderation: Preventing harassment, spam, bots, underage accounts, and malicious activity.
  • App Reliability: Diagnosing technical bugs, analyzing performance, and improving features.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers to deliver our services:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud Platform: Secure authentication, Firestore encrypted cloud database, and Firebase Cloud Storage.
  • Google Mobile Ads: Delivering relevant in-app advertisements in compliance with privacy regulations.

These providers only process data as necessary to perform their respective services on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You may permanently delete your account at any time:

To delete your account:

Open Matching Bird → Navigate to Profile → Tap Settings tab → Tap Delete Account Permanently.

Upon confirmation, your profile, photos, conversations, and account records will be permanently removed from our active database.

5. Security of Your Information

We implement robust technical and administrative security measures, including HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 cloud encryption at rest, to safeguard your data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

6. Age Requirement (Strictly 18+)

Matching Bird is exclusively intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that an account belongs to a minor, we will immediately terminate the account and delete all associated data.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, inquiries, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact our privacy team:
