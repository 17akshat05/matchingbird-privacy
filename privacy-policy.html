2 Information We Collect and Why

We collect customer information solely to operate, personalize, and secure the dating and matching experience. Information is grouped into the following categories:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Authentication & Identity Identifiers: Unique user identifier (UID), email address, and authentication provider metadata via Google OAuth, Email/Password, or Guest Sign In.
  • Profile Details: Display name, age / date of birth, gender, connection preferences (e.g. Man, Woman, Everyone), written personal bio, interest tags, and responses to profile prompt cards.
  • Uploaded Photos & Gallery Media: Profile photos uploaded to your gallery and selected avatar personalization frames.
  • Interactions & Messages: Right/left swipes, mutual matches, chat messages, game invitations, and in-app rivalry events.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Geographical Location: With your permission, your device's approximate GPS coordinates are converted into an encrypted geohash to calculate distance to potential matches within your discovery radius (e.g., 10km to 100km). You can also manually choose a discovery city or adjust distance visibility in Settings.
  • Device & Telemetry Information: Device model, manufacturer, Android/Fire OS version, app version code, screen dimensions, and diagnostic crash logs to maintain stability, prevent bots, and ensure multi-device synchronization.

3 Authentication & Guest Account Architecture

MatchingBird supports three secure authentication methods:

  1. Google Sign-In: Secure OAuth 2.0 flow verifying your identity via Google Identity Services without exposing your Google account password.
  2. Email & Password Sign-In: Secure email authentication with PBKDF2/Argon2 cloud password hashing.
  3. Guest Sign-In / Reviewer Access: Creates an isolated anonymous or device-backed Firebase authentication session. Guest sessions allow exploring the app without sharing an email address. Guest accounts have the same strict data isolation and can be permanently deleted at any time.

5 Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data. We retain your personal customer information only for as long as your account remains active.

How to permanently delete your account & data inside the app:

1. Open MatchingBird.

2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation.

3. Tap the Settings gear or tab.

4. Scroll to the Account Management section and tap Delete Account Permanently.

5. Confirm your deletion in the confirmation dialog.

What occurs upon account deletion:

  • Your user document and profile record in Cloud Firestore are permanently erased.
  • All gallery photos stored in Supabase Cloud Storage are permanently purged from the storage bucket.
  • Your active chats, matches, likes, and swipe history are permanently invalidated and removed.
  • Your Firebase Authentication account is deleted, revoking all active session tokens immediately.

6 Security Practices & Encryption

We deploy defense-in-depth security practices:

  • Data in Transit: All client-to-backend communications utilize HTTPS/TLS 1.3 cryptographic transport.
  • Data at Rest: Cloud databases and object storage volumes are encrypted with AES-256 standard encryption.
  • Row-Level Security & Granular Rules: Cloud Firestore security rules enforce that users can only read or modify records belonging to their authenticated UID. Private chat messages are restricted strictly to the two matched participants.
  • Zero Client Secrets: No service-role keys or master API secrets are bundled in the mobile application.
